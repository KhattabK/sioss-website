About SIOSS

Established in Sweden.
Built for the World.

Founded in Sweden in 2015, SIOSS is a specialized educational institution dedicated to the training and development of sports coaches across various disciplines. We apply the latest teaching methodologies and interactive technologies to deliver an exceptional learning experience.

Our mission is to transfer modern sports technology and knowledge to the Arab region and beyond, providing a high-quality educational environment that meets international standards in sports science and coaching.
