# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: aspiring and working coaches and personal trainers in the Middle East (Iraq, Bahrain and the wider Arab region) who are considering a SIOSS course or certification. They arrive wanting proof that SIOSS is a serious, accredited institution before they get in touch. Content is bilingual (English / Arabic, RTL).

## Product Purpose
SIOSS (Swedish International Organization of Sport Science), founded in Sweden in 2015 and based in Linköping, trains and certifies sports coaches (personal trainer, football fitness, strength & mobility, HRV). The site exists to build trust and turn visitors into enquiries via the contact form, phone or social channels. It also hosts a public graduate registry (students.html) backed by Supabase, managed via admin.html.

## Positioning
A Swedish-founded sport-science institution that brings European coaching education to the Arab region, recognised by Iraqi universities and Svenska Fotbollsförbundet.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS on Vercel; videos served from Cloudflare R2; students from Supabase.
- EN/AR language switch via i18n.js (data-i18n keys) — any new copy needs both languages.
- Contact form posts to Formspree.

## Brand Commitments
- Existing logo (images/logo-white.png, images/logo.jpeg) and current identity are kept (refinement, not rebrand).

## Evidence on Hand
- Certificates/recognitions: images/cert_*.jpg (SvFF, Univ. of Basrah, Baghdad, Baghdad Women, Samarra, Wasit, Ashur).
- Course photos: images/bahrain*.jpg, images/iraq*.jpg, images/gallery*.jpg.
- Instructor photos and names (index.html #team), student testimonial videos and highlight reels on R2, HRV YouTube video.
- Graduate registry in Supabase.
- No verified statistics: do NOT show figures like class counts, satisfaction rates or community size.

## Product Principles
1. Proof over claims: certificates, real photos, real graduates and named instructors carry the persuasion.
2. Equal quality in English and Arabic.
3. Make getting in touch effortless.
