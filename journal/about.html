About the journal

Aims, scope and policies

What the journal publishes, how it decides, and the terms under which articles are made available.

Aim and mission

The SIOSS Journal of Sport Science is an electronic, peer-reviewed scientific journal addressing current issues in sport, published with the goal of advancing sport knowledge through research and expertise.

SJSS seeks to provide readers with up-to-date information comprising advances in all areas of sport. Published articles include both detailed scientific methods and guided conclusions geared towards sports professionals, allowing for immediate application in the field.

Scope

  • Sports management
  • Sports coaching
  • Sports studies
  • Sports medicine
  • Sports fitness and health

Types of articles

  • Original research papers
  • Reviews and mini-reviews
  • Short research communications
  • Technical notes
  • Letters to the editor

Criteria for publication

To be published in SJSS, a paper should:

  1. Provide strong evidence for its conclusions.
  2. Be novel.
  3. Be of importance to scientists in sport science.
  4. Ideally, be interesting to researchers in other disciplines related to sport science.
  5. Represent an advance in understanding likely to influence thinking in sport science.

The main criteria for accepting a manuscript are originality, innovation, and scientific importance and interest. To promote international collaboration, the journal encourages submissions that present the results of collaborative research from more than one country.

Open access

Every article published by SJSS is open access: the full text is freely available to readers on this website, without subscription or registration. The journal ensures that published articles remain properly displayed and available to readers.

Funding and commercial policy

The journal’s activities are financed by publication fees paid by authors. SJSS does not accept donations, grants or advertisements from commercial organisations, in order to keep the publication of research unbiased.

Publication fees

The publication fee is USD 100 per paper. Payment details are sent to the author by email. For questions, contact the editorial office at hello@sioss.se.

Publication ethics

Each manuscript must be accompanied by a statement that it has not been submitted for publication elsewhere. Reviewers who believe that submitted material is not original or has been plagiarised are asked to alert the handling editor.
