About the journal

The SIOSS Journal of Sport Science (SJSS) is published with the goal of advancing sport knowledge through research and expertise. It encompasses multiple facets of sport, including sports management, sports coaching, sports studies, sports medicine, and sports fitness and health.

Published articles include both detailed scientific methods and guided conclusions geared towards sports professionals, allowing for immediate application in the field.
